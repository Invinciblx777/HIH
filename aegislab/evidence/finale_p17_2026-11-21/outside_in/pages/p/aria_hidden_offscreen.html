Field notes: how small towns in the Himalayan foothills manage water

Most hill towns rely on a mix of spring-fed tanks, rooftop rainwater harvesting and seasonal streams. During the monsoon, catchment tanks fill within weeks; in the dry months from March to June, towns ration supply by ward.

Three practices stand out: maintaining traditional stone spouts (dhara), mapping recharge zones before building, and metering bulk supply to hotels separately from households. Where these are combined, summer shortages fall sharply.

Challenges remain: tourism peaks coincide with the driest weeks, and new construction often seals recharge areas.
